## Unreleased

- **Docs reframed as an injury engine.** README, CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, `docs/developers/overlay.md`, `docs/reference/json-keys.md`, `release-guide.md` and the `goodprovider.injuryengine` manifest (tagline, description, tags) now describe per-part injury tracking for all damage, with torture and the padded whip as sources; README settings table lists every dashboard key.
- **Injury log and narration.** `Injury_Engine.log` gets one `torture:` or `health:` info line per point (`point:before+added`, `level:light|medium|severe`, `amount`, `health` %), plus `narration:` and `short_event:` for text sent to SkyrimNet. `Engine.LogLine`, `Main.ShortEvent`, `DirectNarrate` logging. `Hit` / `HitPart` take an attacker; `HitUnarmed` after `TakeHits` for bludgeoning fists. Scene and `HitText` use `narration.form` and shared `injuries/session.json` hit templates ("{attacker} hits {target} with {form} …").
- **Torture scene.** `Main.NarrateHits` keeps one scene of (attacker, victim) pairs (StorageUtil parallel lists `KEY_SC_*` on the quest). `SceneTick` (in `OnUpdate`) sends combined lines built by the new native `Engine.SceneText(phase, attackers, victims, sources, masks, levels)` (`InjuryEngine::SceneText`, templates in `injuries/session.json` and each torture json's `narration.session`): `start` (new pairs only), `again` (every `injury_engine.narration.torture_direct_narration_gap`, default 6 s), `switch` (a pair changes method), `end` (no hit for `injury_engine.narration.torture_maximum_break`, default 3 s). The player is attacker or victim: `PurgeDialogue(true)` + DirectNarration, otherwise `Main.OptionalDirectNarrate` (DirectNarration when the speech queue is empty, else a short-lived event). Only pairs in sight are narrated; out of sight pairs end with one `RegisterPersistentEvent` between torturer and tortured. Removed `narration.summary.end`, `TORTURE_END_DELAY`, `KEY_SESSION_*`, `EndTortureSession`, the per-type sessions and the interval / window batching (`KEY_PENDING_*`, `ClearBatch`), `injury_engine.narration.torture_narration_interval`, `Actions.NarrateSummary`. `torture_narration_window_size` now only gates passive healing text.
- **No effect-list walk in damage hooks.** `Classifier::ClassifyActiveTorture` walked `VisitActiveEffects` from the `ModActorValue` / `HandleHealthDamage` hooks, which run inside the engine's own active-effect update; when effects were dispelled in that update (e.g. removing a Devious Devices armbinder) the nested walk read a dead node and crashed (`SkyrimSE+0FAEDF5`, read 0x1). Torture effects are now recorded as they land (`Classifier::NoteEffect` from the AddTarget hook, and torture-spell hit events) and read back within `kTortureWindow` (2 s).
- **`External_TortureText`.** A silent `External_Damage` for torture types that returns the injuries' feeling text (`Main.TortureText`: `HitPart` hits, `Engine.HitText`) instead of narrating, `""` when no hit landed. SkyrimNet_SexLab Punish narrates it with its own line.
- **Case-insensitive names.** `InjuryEngine::PartIndex` and `InjuryConfig::IndexOf` compare with `InjuryConfig::IEquals`: SkyrimNet_SexLab's `"pussy"` arrived as `"Pussy"` (BSFixedString pool) and the hit landed on a random part.
- **No passive heal events inside a torture session.** `Main.InTortureSession` (last torture hit within `injury_engine.narration.torture_narration_window_size`): `NarrateHealing` drops its passive event and `NarrateSourceHeal` its no-source event; healing itself is unchanged.
- **`External_Damage(attacker, victim, cause, body_part, damage_type, damage_amount, narrate)`.** Preferred external API: always applies the damage; `narrate` uses the normal torture narration rules with `cause` leading the line or event in place of our own "as {attacker}" / "by the fall" clause. `narrate = false` is silent. Real-damage types narrate `cause` as a short-lived event. `External_Strike` / `External_Torture` / `External_Injure` are kept for older callers. `HitText` takes `noCause`.
- **Injury types from other mods.** Any `injuries/<id>.json` another mod ships (merged by the mod manager) loads like ours; documented in `injuries/guide.md`. SkyrimNet_Leashed ships `leash_dragging.json`.
- **`model: "both"`.** One file, a torture and a health source: shared keys plus `torture` / `health` blocks merged over them; the health half's id is `<id>_real`. `InjuryConfig::IndexOf(base, torture)`, `Source::base`; `sexual_parts.json` text falls back to the file id.
- **`falling.json`** (`both`): torture "Falls and Scrapes", health "Fall Injuries". `Classifier`: damage with no hit event and no attacker while `bhkCharacterController::fallTime > 0` → `falling_real`.
- **`External_Injure(attacker, victim, text, type, count, part, narrate, torture)`**; `External_Torture` wraps it. Natives `Engine.RealHit(actor, attacker, source, points)` (`InjuryEngine::RealHit`, through `OnDamage`) and `Engine.HasHealth(source)`.
- **Torture damage replaces the whip model.** `model: "torture"` (`"whip"` read as an alias) for `whipping` plus new `pinching`, `slapping`, `pulling`, `punching`, `fire`, `frost`, `shock` (`injuries/*.json`, with `part_weights`, `hook.hit_share`, `action.verb` / `action.animation`; `sexual_parts.json` gains a text block per type). `InjuryEngine` keeps one deficit per (type, part); a part counts its largest type (health hold, overlay); healing lowers every type on the part by one; real-damage replacement lowers the maximum and clamps the other types. Co-save record 4 (2 / 3 still load). `kMaxSources` 32.
- **Hooked torture.** `Classifier`: unarmed NPC hits with the attacker out of combat → `punching`; the "(torture)" spells (SPEL 0x816–0x818 Flames / Frostbite / Sparks (torture), MGEF 0x813–0x815 copied from vanilla, matched by form, no combat check; vanilla spells are always burning / freezing / shocking) → `fire` / `frost` / `shock` (hit event cause, attacker, or an active-effect scan). `Main.GiveGear` gives the whip and the three spells when `injury_engine.action.give_to_player` ("Add gear to player") is on; Main properties `TortureFlames` / `TortureFrostbite` / `TortureSparks`. `OnDamage(..., melee)` returns 0 for torture and adds torture hits (melee one, spells one per `hook.hit_share` of max health), narrated through `TakeHits`.
- **Action.** `Whip_Target` → `Torture_Target` (`type: whip|pinch|slap|pull|punch`); `Main.Torture`, `Main.External_Torture`; native `IsTorture`. `NarrateInjury` batches every torture type; non-whip torture events are type `torture_hit`.
- **Torture narration window / interval.** Settings `injury_engine.narration.torture_narration_window_size` (30) and `.torture_narration_interval` (6), defaults in `whipping.json` `defaults`. `NarrateInjury` (non-fresh torture hits) uses the time since StorageUtil `injury_engine.last_torture` (`Main.KEY_LAST_TORTURE`, `SinceLastTorture`; also stamped by `Actions.NarrateSummary`): over the window clears the batch and narrates the hit, between interval and window narrates the batch, under the interval a short-lived event; any narration needs an empty speech queue, else a short-lived event. `injury_engine.narration.cooldown` / `NarrationAllowed` now gate healing only.
- **Overlay / settings.** Title "Torture Damage"; dashboard labels say torture (keys renamed to `injury_engine.*` with the Injury_Engine rename).

https://github.com/GoodProvider/SkyrimNet_Whip/releases/tag/0.1.0

First release. Requires SkyrimNet 0.25.0 (Beta 25) or later, SKSE, Address Library, PapyrusUtil SE and PrismaUI.

- **A padded whip for SkyrimNet.** Craft it at the forge or add it with the console; every real hit with it lashes the target.
- **Wounds on a random body part.** Face, back, arms and legs each take damage from light to severe, and the welt decal shows on the skin.
- **Wounds heal.** Over game time, and with healing spells, potions and natural regeneration, parts move through recovering and mostly healed to healed (scarred). Health stays held down while you are wounded.
- **NPCs react.** Hits and healing are narrated to the LLM, batched so a flurry of lashes does not flood it. Everyone who looks at the victim knows what the wounds look like, and the victim knows how they feel.
- **NPCs can whip someone.** The new `Whip_Target` action walks to the target and lashes them several times; by default the NPC must be carrying the whip.
- **Whipping overlay.** Shows a bar per wounded actor, damage first, then healing progress; it fades out by itself.
- **Other mods can use it.** SkyrimNet_SexLab's Force wounds the chosen body part and its Aid heals it, with the narration from Injury_Engine. Mod authors: see the External API in `docs/developers/papyrus.md`.
- **Settings.** Narration cooldown and range, strike limit, healing speed and regeneration, giving yourself Healing Hands, and the overlay are in the SkyrimNet dashboard under Injury_Engine.
