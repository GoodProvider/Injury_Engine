This mod has not shipped. Rename every name that means this mod. Do not keep the old plugin name, old setting paths, or old sibling symbols as fallbacks.

**Code identifier `Whipped`** (when it means this mod) → **`InjuryEngine`**: namespaces, files, functions, log tags, `toWhipped`, `feelingFromWhipped`, `WhippedInstalled`, `WhippedInjure`, `WhippedFallDamage`, `onWhippedSexualParts`.

- `Do you wish to enable WHIPPED sexual parts?` → `Do you wish to enable torture sexual parts?`
