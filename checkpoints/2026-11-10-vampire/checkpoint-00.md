Blood Magic (Hemomancy)
Because blood is their life source, vampires frequently use it as both a weapon and a spellcasting component.

Life Drain (Vampiric Touch): Siphoning a victim's life force or health to heal the vampire's own wounds, even without biting them.

Blood Boiling/Puppetry: Manipulating the blood inside a living target to cause internal damage or force them to move against their will.

Thrall Creation: Feeding a mortal a drop of vampiric blood to grant them enhanced strength and bind them into fanatical servitude (often called ghouls or familiars)
