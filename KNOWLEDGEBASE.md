# KNOWLEDGEBASE

Quirks and lessons for Injury_Engine. Read before changes; append a dated entry (newest first) after a session that found something non-obvious. Entries marked *(from SkyrimNet_SexLab)* were learned there and apply here too.

## The reply gate starved death lines in combat (2026-10-20, needs in-game verification)

From `checkpoints/to-quite/logs/2026-10-20-11-36-51/`: all 4 deaths had DLL text, but every one went out as an `injury_death` event. A killing blow usually lands 1–2 s after a hit DirectNarration on the same pair. That narration asks the victim to reply, and the victim is now dead. In combat, barks keep the speech queue busy and reset the audio-ended time, so `ClaimNarration` saw neither "replied" nor 15 s of "silence", and the single try fell back to the event. Fix (Papyrus): the gate opens when `last_direct_target` is dead, and a gated death is held for 1 s (`DeathTick`, `KEY_DP_*`) before becoming the event. `ClaimNarration` now logs a closed gate (verbose, at most once a second), and hits log which check failed (`mode` / `cooloff` / `not visible` / `gate`).

## Hits keep landing for a moment at or below zero health, before IsDead (2026-10-20, needs in-game verification)

From `checkpoints/2026-10-20-injury-bands/logs/2026-10-20-09-54-07/Injury_Engine.log` lines 2990–3014: on Feduzdal, a burning hit took health from 5 to -25, which was the killing blow. After it, a piercing hit (-55 → -115, 172 real points) and then a 0.1 nipple sliver still reached `OnDamage`, because `victim->IsDead()` was false. The last hit overwrote `g_lastReal`, so the death text said `piercing part:nipples`. Fix: `OnDamage` (health model) ignores a hit when health before it is at or below 0. Health before = `GetActorValue(kHealth) + amount` when `applied` (HandleHealthDamage, after the damage), else the current value. Torture is not affected.

## An unwounded actor lost its real carry after every hit (2026-10-18, needs in-game verification)

Found in `bug-logs/spell-damage-xamos/03-Injury_Engine.log`: four Iron Sword hits (3.0 health each) on Skadi (max health 550, sexual parts on, one real point = 550 / 165 ≈ 3.33 health) each logged `no new point (carry 2.96)`, and vanilla Flames ticks (0.04) logged `carry 0.04` 366 times. `OnDamage` builds a fresh `State` for an unwounded actor, puts the hit into `st.carry`, and when no point lands `!AnyInjury(st)` erases the State with its carry. So an unwounded actor was only wounded by a single hit worth at least one point: small weapons, high-health actors and damage over time never started a wound. Fix: the carry of an erased State goes to `g_realCarry` (not saved, cleared by `Erase`, load and revert); the next hit on that actor seeds `st.carry` with it, clamped to the health the Health `kDamage` modifier holds minus this hit (health still missing before it; `HandleHealthDamage` runs after the damage, a path that runs before it only undercounts). Not `maxHealth - health`: a temporary Health buff can put health above the permanent max (Skadi at 579.96 / 550 in `checkpoints/spell-damage-xamos/logs/01-Injury_Engine.log`), which made that negative and reset the carry to 0 on every hit. Debug line `carry … from the last hit, … still missing -> …`, so regen and healing drain it. Debug line `carry … kept for the next hit`. Torture already had the same thing (`g_tortureCarry`).

## A buff wearing off read as a fall or a wound (2026-10-18, verified in game for Courage, log 03)

Player report: a follower sneaking along got "Camilla Valerius stumbles." every time the player's Ocato's Recital buffs (Courage, Nightfall) wore off. The supplied logs had no Injury_Engine lines; the diagnosis comes from the code and a local log.
- **`bhkCharacterController::fallStartHeight` is stale between falls.** A standing actor logged `fallTime:0.00 drop:-12613`. The old test (`fallTime > 0` and `drop >= min_drop`) accepted any no-cause Health loss during a brief `fallTime` (a step, a slope) whenever the old start height sat 300+ units above the actor. `ClassifyFall` now also needs `fallTime >= 0.3 s` and `drop <= 686 × max(gravity, 1) × t² + 100` (twice free fall plus slack; `gravity` is the controller member at 0x248, a multiplier). Rejections log the reason at debug; accepted landings log `landing ...` at info.
- **A buff ending is not a wound.** A Recover-flagged Health effect with a negative change (`ValueModifierEffect::ModifyActorValue` 0x20, non-torture only) or a negative `kTemporary` Health `ModActorValue` marks the actor (`Classifier::NoteBuffChange`). Attacker-less Health damage inside that effect call (`BuffScope`) or within 1 s of the mark passes through with no classification, and `ResolveUncaused` drops a held chunk with a mark within 2.5 s. Always-on line: `[buff] <hook> <id> ... not a wound` (throttled).
- **Answered by the 0.1.2 repro (`bug-logs/spell-damage-xamos/02-*`):** a mod Courage (MGEF 851E56CE, re-applied every second by Ocato's Recital) ending reached **only** an attacker-less `HandleHealthDamage(-30)`: no 0x20 Recover call, no vtable `kTemporary` change, so no mark, and `ResolveUncaused` landed `stuck:30` (top = health before = 115, health 85/85) as a severe generic wound. Fix, independent of the effect archetype: `HandleHealthDamage` runs after the damage, so real damage is already in the Health `kDamage` modifier; when the reported loss exceeds `-GetActorValueModifier(kDamage, kHealth)` by more than 0.5, the loss is max health shrinking and passes through as a buff change (`[buff] ... shrink:true`, also marks the actor). `ResolveUncaused` caps `top` at the current max health. A real no-cause loss in the same call as a buff ending is skipped (accepted).
- **Verified (`bug-logs/spell-damage-xamos/03-Injury_Engine.log`):** Courage on Skadi, refreshed and worn off: `[buff] HandleHealthDamage ... value:-30 ... damageMod:0.0 shrink:true` and `value:-25 ... health:580.0/550.0 ... shrink:true`, no `uncaused ... landed`, no wound. The health left above max after the buff is pulled down by `SyncHealth` on the next wound (an internal `ModActorValue`), which is expected.

## Review v02 fixes: Papyrus threads, stale sessions, swing-based melee (2026-10-18, needs in-game verification)

Every native this DLL registers runs on the game thread, so the calling Papyrus thread yields, and so does any call on an actor (`IsDead`, `GetDistance`). Another `OnInjury` / `OnUpdate` thread on the Main quest can run at each of those points. Rules that came out of the review:
- **Take + read native pairs need a drain flag.** `hits_draining`, `sync_draining` (SyncHealth / TakeSyncEvents → SyncActors / SyncCasters) and `deaths_draining` (TakeDeaths → DeathVictims / ...). `OnUpdate` skips its `SyncHealth` for that tick while another thread drains. An empty take never clears the C++ result arrays. Those arrays hold FormIDs now, resolved in the read native, so a deleted summon is `None`, not a dangling pointer.
- **Scene / heal indices go stale across any yield.** Find the pair again by its actors (`SceneIndexOf`, `HealFind`) after a yielding call before writing to its lists.
- **Real-time stamps don't survive a relaunch.** `Setup` drops every torture pair and heal session (`ClearSessions`), and a stamp in the future counts as expired. `KEY_IN_ACTION` has a refreshed stamp (`in_action_time`), so a load mid-`Torture_Target` no longer swallows that NPC's real lashes.
- **ProcessHitData classifies the swing**, `HitData.weapon` (null = fists), through `Classifier::ClassifyMelee`, never the equipped gear or a live spell effect. Its "already handled" mark is per victim, cross-thread and used once (`MarkHandled` / `TakeHandled`).
- **A stored Torture Padded Whip hit event no longer answers every damage call in its 0.6 s window.** `Classify` falls through to the hands loop, the live torture effect and drowning.
- **Classify rules are resolved to FormIDs once in `Classifier::Init`.** Damage hooks compare ids. The old by-name `LookupForm` walked the whole file list on every tick.
- **Queued combat stops re-check** the calm / truce on the game thread, so `Start_Fighting` (`ForgetCalm` / `ForgetTruces`) in between wins. `kPreLoadGame` / `kNewGame` call `Classifier::ResetSession` (truces, hit / effect / calm caches, friend-hit flags). A background timer releases friend-hit flags when the sync loop is not running.
- **Truce answers carry a ticket.** A late LLM or message-box answer for a request that timed out, was replaced, or belongs to an earlier load is ignored.
- **Overlay pushes carry a sequence number** and the page drops older ones. A reset is the same `injuryEngineUpdate` call (`reset` true).
- **Config hardening.** A wrong field type skips that classify rule or that source's hook block, not the whole load. A non-bool `npc` / `combat` skips the rule. `overlay.order` is range-checked. Every part's region index is checked after load (missing `sexual_parts.json`).
- With sexual parts on, `ActiveParts()` is 11, so the lungs are never a torture part or in the health capacity. Drowning still reaches them through `PartActive`.
- **Not changed:** the release DLL stays `/MT`. The UCRT allocates from the process heap, so a SkyrimNet `std::string` freed here is safe in release (SexLab ships the same way). Debug builds still skip those calls (`CrossDllStdStringSafe`).

## Freeze: g_lock held across VisitEffects (2026-10-16, needs in-game verification)

The game froze (no crash log) while Bob held Torture Flames on Angi just after she went into torture bleedout. `Injury_Engine.log` and `Papyrus.0.log` stopped at the same second, but `SkyrimNet.log` threads kept writing for 15 s, so the game thread was blocked rather than crashed. Likely an ABBA deadlock. The 1 s healing scan task (`SyncHealth` → SKSE task) took `InjuryEngine::g_lock` and then walked the actor's effects (`MagicTarget::VisitEffects`, the engine's effect list lock). A torture tick runs inside the engine's effect update and waits for `g_lock` in the `ModifyEffectActorValue` hook (`OnDamage`). Fix: `CollectHealingEffects` walks the effects without `g_lock` and copies caster handle + item, and only then does the task lock and call `Seen`. `NoteHealingSource` also sends its mod event after unlocking. Rule: never hold `g_lock` (or Classifier's locks) across an engine call that takes engine locks (effect walks, casting, `SendEvent`). If a freeze happens again, turn on verbose logging and check the last line; the next suspects are `ConvertHealth` / `TakeHealth` changing health under `g_lock`.

## Torture Padded Whip weapon hits are zeroed in ProcessHitData (2026-10-15, needs in-game verification)

The whip's own weapon hit was real damage: `Classify` returns `-1` (ignore) for it, so `ProcessHitData` skipped it and `HandleHealthDamage` kept all of it. Base damage is 1, but perks, power attacks and sneak attacks multiply it, and it lands below the torture floor, so a tortured victim could be whipped to death the same way Angi was punched to death in v07. Fix (v09): `ProcessHitData` also zeroes a hit with ignore result + `physicalMelee` (no `OnDamage`; the lash ENCH → `OnLash` brings the torture), and `HandleHealthDamage` gives back anything that still lands (`HandledByProcessHit`). Not verified: whether the lash ENCH still applies when `totalDamage` is 0 before the original call. If lashes stop landing, remove the zeroing and keep only the restore.

## Torture punches are zeroed in ProcessHitData; torture bleedout (2026-10-15, needs in-game verification)

A torture punch killed Angi (12:57 run). Every punch was `punching`, but the last one was a power attack (`amount:20.0`) at 5/150 health. It killed her before `HandleHealthDamage` could give the health back (the entry below). Health had also dropped below the 10% floor (21.9 → 14.0 → 5.0) when `TakeHealth` could take nothing, so part of the punch damage was not being given back. The path is unknown because verbose logging was off. Fix (v08): a call hook at `RelocationID(37673, 38627)` + `VariantOffset(0x3C0, 0x4A8)` (the `ProcessHitData(Actor*, HitData&)` call that D7ry's combat mods hook; guarded by the `E8` opcode check, skipped on VR) classifies the hit. For a torture melee hit it calls `OnDamage` with `HitData.totalDamage`, then zeroes `totalDamage`, `physicalDamage` and `bonusHealthDamageMult` before the original runs. A `HandleHealthDamage` call for that hit within 100 ms on the same thread only restores health (`HandledByProcessHit`). Also new: torture bleedout. The animation events `BleedOutStart` / `BleedOutStop` go through `NotifyAnimationGraph` on an SKSE task, and graph bool `IsBleedingOut` is used to put a victim back down if the AI stood them up. Not verified: whether the graph variable exists on every skeleton (if `GetGraphVariableBool` fails, nothing is re-sent), and how the player behaves as the victim.

## A lethal real hit at the floor kills before torture is replaced (accepted, 2026-10-15)

A sword hit on Angi, held at the 10% floor by torture (15/150), killed her: `death ... under torture, last real hit: none`, no `health:` line. `HandleHealthDamage` reports health already taken, so a hit that is lethal by itself kills before the hook runs, and `OnDamage` returns early for a dead victim (nothing replaced or logged). Kill moves can do the same. Accepted for now. Possible fix if ever wanted: cap `HitData.totalDamage` to `health - 1` for tortured victims in the `ProcessHitData` call (37673 / 38627; verify the offsets) and settle the overflow in `HandleHealthDamage`.

## ValueModifierEffect::ModifyActorValue gets kNone as its actor value (2026-10-15)

The vfunc 0x20 hook on `VTABLE_ValueModifierEffect` / `VTABLE_DualValueModifierEffect` is called on every tick, but its `a_actorValue` argument is always `kNone` (`av:-1` in the log). The effect's real value is the member `ValueModifierEffect::actorValue` (0x90, set by `SetActorValue`, slot 0x19): 24 (Health) for the torture MGEFs. Filtering on the argument let every tick through; read `a_av != kNone ? a_av : this->actorValue`. Confirmed in game (12:03 run): torture Flames / Frostbite / Sparks ticks were blocked (`change:+0.000`) with the real caster as attacker.

## HandleHealthDamage runs after the damage: torture spells killed (2026-10-15)

Torture Flames killed Angi four times, also from full health with no whip. A probe logged health before / after the original `Actor::HandleHealthDamage` call for torture ticks: health was **already lower on entry** (148.7, 138.4, 128.7 … one call per second) and the original call took **0**. `HandleHealthDamage` reports a health change already made; the argument we zeroed changed nothing, and the spell's full damage (~7%/s) went through while torture points landed at 1–2 a second. `ActorValueOwner::ModActorValue` (slot 6) never saw these ticks at all. The "Someone" attacker comes from `HandleHealthDamage` with a null attacker. First fix attempt (11:21 run, Angi died again): a hook on `ValueModifierEffect::ModifyActorValue` (vfunc 0x20 on `VTABLE_ValueModifierEffect` and `VTABLE_DualValueModifierEffect`) seemed to block nothing; probes later showed it *is* called and the MGEF match (and AddTarget's `NoteEffect`) works, but the AV argument is `kNone` (entry above). Current fix (11:27 build): `HandleHealthDamage` is called on every tick with the amount just taken, so after the original call it **gives back** `amount - kept` (`InjuryEngine::RestoreHealth`, internal scope). Torture ticks are fully restored (`TakeHealth` applies the floor-limited loss), and real hits get back the share torture wounds absorbed, which also covers punches and the "real damage replaces torture wounds" reduction. Limits: a tick that is lethal by itself has already killed before the notification; a tick classified wrongly (`generic` on the first tick after a re-cast, before any hit event) is not restored beyond the torture share. **Confirmed** (11:38 run: Angi lived, every tick restored). Final state (11:52 build, confirmed 12:03): torture spell ticks are blocked at the effect hook before they land, so they no longer reach `HandleHealthDamage`, the re-cast `generic` tick is gone, and the restore stays as the safety net for punches, real hits replacing torture, and any tick the effect hook misses. Diagnostics are now verbose-only `[hook] ... torture` / `[hook] effect` lines.

## Torture Padded Whip in one hand let a torture spell in the other kill (2026-10-15)

Superseded by the entry above (the real cause). Torture Flames killed a whipped NPC: health went from 10.6% (the floor holds at 10%) to death in about 3 s. `Injury_Engine.log` had the classified `fire` torture lines but no `health:` line and no `death ... by` line. ## Torture is not aggression: spell flags and the whip's combat start (2026-10-15)

A spell counts as hostile when any of its effects has the MGEF `Hostile` flag, including hidden perk effects. The torture spells (0x816–0x818) carry vanilla Flames / Frostbite / Sparks perk and slow effects (Intense Flames fear 0F392F, Frost slow 0B729D, Deep Freeze 0F3934, Disintegrate 0F3F0C), all `Hostile`. These are now non-hostile copies 0x822–0x825 (conditions, scripts, art unchanged; copied from a Spriggit dump of Skyrim.esm, written with English-only `Name`), and 0x813–0x815 lost `Hostile` too. Keep `Detrimental` on any health effect: `IsHealing` treats a health ValueModifier that is not detrimental as healing. The Torture Padded Whip already had WEAP `NonHostile`, yet a whipped NPC still entered combat: the WEAP flag does not cover melee hits. The real decision is `GetFactionFightReaction(victim, attacker)` called from the hit handler (SE 37672 +0x187, AE 38626 +0x182, the site Simple Offence Suppression hooks): neutral = the hit is an attack. The DLL now answers ally for whip lashes and sets `kIgnoreFriendlyHits` on the victim (released 3 s after the last lash and before saves), so no combat starts. The calm below came first and stays as the fallback: it worked, but a frame late (combat visibly started, then stopped). The DLL now calms it: `Classifier` records each torture hit (hit sink: whip or a torture spell; damage hook: every torture-classified hit) and a `TESCombatEvent` sink stops the victim's combat (`StopCombat` + `StopCombatAndAlarmOnActor`, SKSE task) when it starts against that attacker within 3 s, in either order (a combat start waits 1 s for the hit). Actors already fighting send no combat start, so they keep fighting. Unverified in game: whether the alarm on the player (bounty) also clears, and whether a friendly victim's allies still join.

## Stop_Fighting / Start_Fighting and the torture calm (2026-10-15)

Papyrus `StopCombat` alone does not stick on a hostile NPC: its Aggression makes it pick the fight up again on the next detection. An accepted `Stop_Fighting` therefore sets Aggression 0 (restored after 24 game hours, or by `Start_Fighting`); a refused one only stops combat, so the fight may resume (by design). The stop is the torture calm's (`StopCombat` + `StopCombatAndAlarmOnActor`), as an SKSE task. The calm itself (entry above) would cancel a `Start_Fighting` issued within 3 s of a torture hit between the same pair (the combat start lands inside `kCalmWindow`), and the lash's `kIgnoreFriendlyHits` flag would keep the target from treating hits as attacks, so `Start_Fighting` calls `ForgetCalm` first. Unverified in game: whether `StartCombat` sticks for a follower ordered against an ally, and whether SkyrimNet finds decision templates under `external/goodprovider.injuryengine/prompts/decisions/`.

## LLM actions are YAML, not Papyrus RegisterAction (2026-10-15)

The four actions are YAML in `external/goodprovider.injuryengine/actions/` (as in SkyrimNet_SexLab). `questEditorId: Injury_Engine` (QUST 0x800), `scriptName: Injury_Engine_Actions`; `executionFunctionName` is a **non-global** member, and `parameterMapping` entries are passed positionally (`speaker` first; a dynamic `target` arrives as `Actor`, other dynamic params as `String`). YAML eligibility only tests the speaker (`currentActor`) and can't use Papyrus decorators, so the functions re-check the player / dead / target. Cost of the switch: `Torture_Target`'s type list and strike range are static (`whip|pinch|slap|pull|punch`, `1..10`); torture types another mod adds through injury JSON (`action.alias`) are not offered by the action, and `max_strikes` only caps. A save that ran the old Papyrus `RegisterAction` may keep those registrations until the game restarts. Unverified in game: that SkyrimNet loads the YAML from this plugin folder and passes the `strikes` string correctly.

## Torture spells: classify MGEF before resist health (2026-10-11)

`Torture` spells list the torture MGEF first and a vanilla resist effect second. `Classifier::Init` resolves `effect_file` / `effect_id` to a runtime form id (`LookupForm`, else ESL `0xFE000000 | (smallFileCompileIndex << 12) | localId`). `MatchMagic` prefers that torture MGEF over resist-only health rules (`shocking`, …); vanilla Flames / Frostbite / Sparks stay health. `OnTortureDamage` lands one point on the first tick of `fire` / `frost` / `shock` beams. `Main.NarrateHits` sends `SceneSend("start", …)` immediately on the first registered hit of any torture source (method change uses `start` again, not `switch`).

## DirectNarration reenters Papyrus during SceneTick (2026-10-11)

`SkyrimNetApi.DirectNarration` can run other scripts (eligibility checks, and so on) before it returns. If `SceneTick` marks torture pairs as started only after `SceneSend`, a nested `OnUpdate` still sees `SC_PENDING` and sends the same `start` line twice. Claim state before `SceneSend` (`SC_RUNNING` for starts, clear `KEY_SC_SWITCH` for method changes) and ignore a nested `SceneTick` with `scene_tick_active`.

## Concentration torture beams use HandleHealthDamage per tick (2026-10-11)

Sparks / Flames / Frostbite `Torture` are concentration spells with a projectile; each tick can arrive in `Actor::HandleHealthDamage`, not only in `ModActorValue`. Those ticks must **not** set `physicalMelee` (they land `hook.points_per_second` points per real second like other spell torture). When the hit form is a projectile or otherwise fails `ClassifyForm`, `Classifier` must prefer the live `Torture` effect from `g_torture` over guessing unarmed `punching` on an out-of-combat NPC with empty hands.

## Never walk active effects from damage hooks (2026-10-10)

`ModActorValue` / `HandleHealthDamage` hooks run inside the engine's own active-effect update (a value-modifier effect applying damage). A nested `VisitActiveEffects` / `GetActiveEffectList` walk there crashes when effects are dispelled in the same update (`SkyrimSE+0FAEDF5`, `mov rax,[rcx]` reading 0x1, `MagicTarget::EffectVisitor` on the stack; seen right after removing a DD armbinder). Track effects as they land instead: `Classifier::NoteEffect` (AddTarget hook) and hit events feed `g_torture`, read back within `kTortureWindow`.

## Torture replaced the whip model; hooked torture needs in-game verification (2026-10-09)

Whipping is now one of eight `model: "torture"` types (`"whip"` is still read as torture). Each (type, part) holds torture points (0 .. part max); a part counts only its **largest** type for the health hold, the overlay and the stage. Healing lowers every type on the part by one (slap 3, pinch 4, fire 1, heal 2 gives 1 / 2 / 0); real damage replacing torture lowers the maximum by one and clamps every type to it (convert 2 gives 2 / 2 / 1). A hit on a type that sits below the part's maximum costs no health. Co-save record 5. Record 4 stored `healing_max (11) + damage`, so it loads as `max(0, deficit - 11)`; records 2 / 3 load as whipping damage only. The old healing range (recovering / mostly healed) is gone: a part healing from severe goes through the heal levels low, mid, high on the same points. Unverified, check the `classify` / `torture damage` lines with verbose logging: (1) fire / frost / shock torture comes only from the "Torture" spells (MGEF 0x813–0x815, copies of vanilla Flames / Frostbite / Sparks effects; matched by form, in or out of combat; vanilla spells are always real damage); (2) concentration spells may tick after the 0.6 s `TESHitEvent` window, so `Classifier` falls back to scanning the victim's active effects for a torture MGEF; (3) an out-of-combat punch is classified before combat starts (the attacker's `IsInCombat()` at the hit); (4) the hooks return 0 for torture, so the game applies no damage and `TakeHealth` applies the floor-limited torture loss instead. **(4) was false for `HandleHealthDamage`; see 2026-10-15.** `kMaxSources` is 32 (8 torture + 8 health files already filled 16).

## PrismaUI view path (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-07-24)

`CreateView("Injury_Engine/overlay.html")` loads from **`Data/PrismaUI/views/`**, not `SKSE/Plugins/`. `make release` copies `PrismaUI/` into the package. A missing file gives a valid-looking C++ path but no visible UI. Do not `Focus` a view that should not pause the game; the overlay is `Show`-only.

## Separate PrismaUI Invokes are not applied in order (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-10-03)

`PrismaUI->Invoke` is async; two Invokes can land in either order. A state change that must not interleave goes out as **one** Invoke calling one JS entry point (here: `injuryEngineUpdate(json)` with the whole state).

## `window.prompt` / `confirm` do nothing in PrismaUI (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-09-27)

Ultralight returns as if cancelled. Build text entry and confirmations in HTML.

## Papyrus default args are filled at the caller's compile (from SkyrimNet_SexLab)

Adding a parameter (even with a default) needs every caller recompiled, or the stale `.pex` logs `Expected N, got M` and the call returns None. Pass every argument in cross-script calls. See [docs/reference/papyrus-rules.md](docs/reference/papyrus-rules.md).

## ESL FormIDs are signed in Papyrus (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-09-15)

`0xFE057894` arrives as `-33195884`. C++ must `static_cast<uint32_t>` before `LookupByID`.

## SKSE native params must use engine types (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-07-25)

Natives take `RE::Actor*`, `RE::BSFixedString`, etc. Mismatched types fail to bind or crash.

## The .pex and the .dll deploy in opposite directions (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-09-23)

`compile: papyrus` writes `.pex` into this repo's `Scripts/`; the DLL build copies into this repo's `SKSE/Plugins/`. Whatever the game loads comes from the MO2 mod folder — confirm the game is reading this folder (not an installed release copy) before concluding a fix "did not work".

## Action eligibility cannot use Papyrus decorators (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-09-14)

`CallDecoratorDirect` returns empty on a cache miss. Use inventory, factions, globals, or Papyrus eligibility functions (as `Whip_IsEligible` does).

## Papyrus runs while a focused PrismaUI view pauses the game (from SkyrimNet_SexLab, 2026-09-28)

Paused game time does not stop Papyrus. Not an issue for the Show-only overlay, but matters if a focused panel is ever added.

## RenderTemplate binds its variable as a string; SkyrimNet inja has no `lower` / `+` (2026-10-04)

`SkyrimNetApi.RenderTemplate(name, "heal", json)` sets `heal` to the JSON *string*: SkyrimNet.log shows `variable 'heal.kind' not found`, the template silently takes its last `else` branch, and the result passes the "Error" / `{{` checks. ## Health ratio must cover the full deficit, or the wounded level goes negative (2026-10-04)

`Deficit()` is damage **plus missing healing** per part, so its maximum is 6 × (20 + 11) = 186, not 6 × 20. With `/ 120`, a deficit of 180 gave `wounded:-35`; the regen take-back pushed health to -21.5 (bleedout), and when health came back (bleedout recovery jumped it to full) the whole deficit converted in one 0.2 s tick: "pain gone" and "fully healed" at once. `Ratio()` now divides by `kPartCount * kMaxDeficit`, and `ConvertHealth` clamps the wounded level to `kHealthFloor`.

## Real damage replaces whip damage; the damage hooks need in-game verification (2026-10-06)

Whip points hold health down by `deficit × 0.9 × max / 186`; real points are worth `max / 120` health each (6 parts × 20 = 100% of max). A real hit while whip points remain is cut by `min(damage, whip hold)` **inside the hook** (the original is called with the reduced amount), the same health worth of whip points is removed and real points added on the same part (one attack = one part, whipped parts first, spilling to another part only when full), so health only drops once all whip wounds are replaced and the 10% floor never blocks real damage. Unverified assumptions, turn on `injury_engine.debug.verbose_logging` and check the `[hook] ...` lines in `Injury_Engine.log`: (1) `Actor::HandleHealthDamage` (vfunc 0x104; the VR slot is not verified, so the hook is skipped on VR) receives the damage as a positive amount (the code uses `fabs` and keeps the sign); (2) melee damage reaches `HandleHealthDamage`, everything else `ActorValueOwner::ModActorValue(kDamage, Health)` (vfunc 6); (3) a nested `ModActorValue` call inside `HandleHealthDamage` is ignored through a thread-local scope, so a damage that reaches both is not counted twice; (4) `TESHitEvent` may fire after the damage, so `Classifier::Classify` falls back to the attacker's equipped weapon, then to unarmed (people bludgeoning, creatures mauling), then `generic`. Damage the engine does itself (`TakeHealth`, regen take-back) is wrapped in `InternalScope` and never classified.

## Injury point log lines are throttled (skipped:N)

## TakeHits / HitVictims race across Papyrus threads (2026-10-11)

`TakeHits` fills a shared C++ buffer that `HitVictims` / `HitAttackers` / `HitUnarmed` read afterwards. Each hooked hit fires `Injury_Engine_Injury`, so several `OnInjury` threads (plus `OnUpdate`) ran `NarrateRealHits` at once. One thread's `TakeHits` cleared the arrays (even when it took nothing) while another was between `TakeHits` and `HitVictims`, so whole batches died with `Array index N is out of range` at `NarrateRealHits`. The first shock batch, the one that sends the scene `start`, was lost this way. Now `TakeHits` returns early without clearing when the queue is empty, and `NarrateRealHits` is guarded by `hits_draining` and loops until `TakeHits` is empty. Any native pair of the form "take + read parallel arrays" needs the same guard.

## Per-hit Papyrus narration lags behind fast hits (2026-10-11)

Every queued hit used to get its own `NarrateRealHits` → `NarrateHits` pass (HitText, scene StorageUtil, visibility, short-lived event). ## Real and torture points are two scales (2026-10-20)

Since co-save version 7 a real (health) point is 1% of max health on any part (`realMax` 100 / 10 / 5), while a torture point stays `woundShare / CapacityTorture` of max health (20-point parts). Never add `RealOnPart` and `PartTorture` directly: torture fullness converts real points by health worth (`PartFullTorture`), real fullness ignores torture (`PartFullReal`, cap = missing health %). The real cap reads the Health `kDamage` modifier, so a caller that takes the health after `OnDamage` must pass `applied = false`, or the hit itself is missing from the cap.

## A SkyrimNet prompt editor override shadows the plugin prompt (2026-10-20)

Saving a prompt in SkyrimNet's dashboard prompt editor writes a copy to `overwrite/SKSE/Plugins/SkyrimNet/overlay/prompts/<same path>` (older copies go to `overlay/_history/`), and that copy wins over `external/goodprovider.injuryengine/prompts/...` from then on. It survives renames and prompt updates. A 2026-10-09 copy of `submodules/character_bio/0420_injury_looks.prompt` still called the pre-rename decorator `skyrimnet_whipped_injuries`, so every bio render logged `variable 'skyrimnet_whipped_injuries' not found` and `Failed to render bio template` (SkyrimNet.log), and wound looks and the healed line never reached the LLM. Fix: remove the overlay file (or revert it in the editor). When a prompt change "does nothing", first look for a same-named file under `overlay/prompts/`.

Unrelated, same log: `MemoryGenerator.cpp ... Failed to parse LLM response as JSON` with a garbage `Raw response` (e.g. `Bob's` after an `Empty completion` failover) is the memory model / provider, not Injury_Engine; SkyrimNet skips that segment.
